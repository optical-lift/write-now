# Relational Law Observation Packet v1 — Pilot Assessment

Status: **PILOT PASSED — INSTRUMENT USABLE FOR PRIOR-MARK HARVEST**
Date: **2026-09-18**
Branch: `mark-relational-law-observation-packet-v1`

## Question

Can one neutral observation container carry materially different Mark findings — including successes, failures, representation limits, field structure, sequence effects, and population sensitivity — without assigning universal laws or forcing the evidence into a closed operator vocabulary?

## Pilot

The pilot contains **27 records** spanning the Mark Engine from the V19–V24 synthesis through V36, V38–V40, V42, V45, V46, V47 and V48.

The selection was deliberately heterogeneous.

It includes:

- failed exact cross-system identity transfer;
- nonrandom organization without successful consequence modeling;
- immediate operator/state consequence;
- context-conditioned consequence;
- additive versus interaction behavior;
- documentary-boundary censoring;
- order sensitivity;
- representation information loss;
- morphology-versus-lemma asymmetry;
- identity-blind sequential signal;
- feature ablation;
- independently frozen external-witness correspondence;
- local-relation overfragmentation;
- external-witness gain that fails anti-lookup generalization;
- center-blind distributed information;
- context-placement null destruction;
- complementary intrinsic and distributed information;
- failed exact centerless-field representation;
- residual same-window association;
- factorized sequential composition;
- repetition/idempotence;
- ordered-pair cancellation;
- pair-specific higher-order residual;
- transferable population-level structural regimes;
- asymmetric scale persistence;
- failure of longer discrete regime history;
- coordinate-map sensitivity to source population.

## Primary result

The packet successfully carried all 27 findings while keeping:

- `universal_law_label = null`;
- `candidate_equivalences = []`;
- `external_semantic_labels_used = []`;
- `cross_source_matching_performed = false`.

No packet required a universal operator name to become meaningful.

Successful and failed findings fit the same custody structure.

This is the central v1 success.

## What the pilot taught us about the required language

### 1. A law-discovery language must represent constraints, not only transformations

Several of the most important Mark findings are not positive operations.

V39, V42, V47 higher-order history and V48 constrain what the future machine is allowed to be.

The packet therefore correctly treats negative evidence as first-class.

### 2. Representation is part of the evidence

V36, V39, V40, V42 and V48 all show that the same underlying observations can behave very differently under different representations.

A future global reconstruction must never erase the question:

> Is the apparent law a property of the evidence, or a property of the coordinate system we chose?

The packet preserves `representation_scope`, used/excluded features, and representation-specific epistemic ceilings for this reason.

### 3. Historical experiment scope and packet independence are different variables

The first pilot draft used a single `source_local_only` flag.

That was wrong.

A prior experiment may legitimately be:

- source-local;
- multi-source but structurally blind;
- cross-system;
- an external-witness comparison;
- a mixed experiment family.

That does not mean the current packet construction performed a new equivalence search.

The revised packet therefore records historical `experiment_scope` separately while requiring `packet_construction_independent = true`.

### 4. Frozen result and later synthesis are different evidence classes

RLOP-001 through RLOP-006 are presently grounded through the preserved V20–V24 post-result synthesis note, while later pilot records cite their frozen result artifacts directly.

The first schema flattened those sources.

The revised schema now requires `evidence.provenance_class`.

This prevents a later harvest from quietly upgrading a synthesis statement into a frozen experimental endpoint.

### 5. Relational description must support more than literal before→after events

V46 and V48 are field/coordinate findings.
V36 contains representation-comparison findings.
V24 contains censoring.
V45 contains true state transitions.

The open `observation_kind` field is therefore preferable to a closed transformation taxonomy.

### 6. Three additional dimensions may deserve promotion later

The pilot repeatedly encountered:

- structured control/null design;
- scale/granularity;
- observation censoring/boundaries.

The v1 schema now has optional sockets for structured comparison and scale coordinates. Boundary/censoring remains representable through relational boundary conditions.

These are not yet mandatory because the pilot does not justify freezing a closed treatment.

## Neutrality check

The packet did **not** need to know:

- Noel's canon-derived laws;
- any proposed natural-world analogue;
- a universal Mark operator vocabulary;
- the B + C whole-system hypothesis as an assignment rule;
- cultural meanings;
- theological meanings;
- language-family relationships.

The whole-system hypothesis motivated the instrument but did not populate the packet with expected global correspondences.

That separation must remain.

## Does the packet destroy important information?

Not obviously at pilot scale.

The strongest evidence that it does not is that the packet can simultaneously preserve apparently conflicting facts:

- exact identity can carry more information than coarse geometry;
- identity-blind structural signal can still generalize;
- local categorical relations can overfit;
- distributed center-blind context can still carry information;
- first-order composition can work strongly;
- pair-specific residual can still remain;
- a fixed regime atlas can transfer strongly;
- an independently rebuilt regime map can still fail coordinate equivalence.

A representation that forced any of these into one favored story would have failed.

v1 preserves them as separate constraints.

## Important limitation

The pilot is **not yet the Prior-Mark Evidence Harvest**.

The 27 records were selected to stress the container, not to exhaust V1–V48.

Early Mark experiments still need direct result-by-result harvesting wherever frozen artifacts exist. The V19–V24 records should also be upgraded from synthesis provenance to direct frozen-result provenance if those exact result artifacts are recoverable.

No absence in this pilot should be interpreted as absence from Mark evidence.

## Decision

**Relational Law Observation Packet v1 passes its pilot purpose.**

It is good enough to begin the next roadmap piece:

> **Prior-Mark Evidence and Constraint Harvest**

That harvest should proceed experiment by experiment, preserving direct frozen provenance wherever available and creating one or more packets for every materially distinct supported, negative, diagnostic, unresolved, or insufficient-contrast finding.

The harvest must remain observation-only.

No cross-source equivalence classes should be created during that work.
