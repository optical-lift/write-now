# Whole-Topology Condition Test v1 — Blind Assessment

Status: **FROZEN BEFORE PROVENANCE REJOIN**  
Date: **2026-09-19**

## Result

The preregistered **pair-density explanation is rejected** on the 705 untouched sources.

This is not a marginal miss. The primary relationship runs in the opposite direction from the hypothesis.

### Primary junction-conditioned endpoint

Eligible untouched sources: **703**

Lowest-density decile: **71** sources  
Blind density boundary: **1.96107056 pair weight per center**

The hypothesis predicted that the lowest-density sources would have **weaker** junction-state matching.

Observed:

- median junction matching lift, low-density: **0.0316411**
- median junction matching lift, remaining sources: **0.0175740**
- low minus rest: **+0.0140671**
- preregistered one-sided permutation p for the predicted negative contrast: **1.0**

The hypothesis also predicted matching lift would increase with density.

Observed:

- Spearman rho: **-0.214860**
- preregistered one-sided p for a positive rho: **1.0**

So pair density does **not** explain the lace exception as a simple condition that weakens the matching law.

It points the other way on average.

## But the failure contains a second, preregistered signal

The secondary reversal diagnostic is strong.

For junction matching:

- negative/reversed lift in lowest-density decile: **22.54%**
- negative/reversed lift in remaining sources: **4.59%**
- difference: **+17.95 percentage points**
- lane-stratified permutation p: **0.00005**

This happens while the **median** low-density matching lift is stronger, not weaker.

That combination matters.

Low density is not simply turning the matching law off.

Instead, the low-density region contains **more of both behaviors**:

- many sources with strong positive matching;
- a much larger minority with actual reversal.

The distribution is becoming more branch-like.

## Lane check

The reversal enrichment is present in all three blind lanes:

- train low-density: **54.17% negative** vs **5.66%** outside its low-density tail;
- control: **20.83%** vs **3.38%**;
- holdout: **8.33%** vs **2.83%**.

But the median-lift effect itself is not stable across lanes:

- train: low-density median is lower;
- control: low-density median is higher;
- holdout: low-density median is higher.

That is another reason not to salvage the simple density hypothesis.

## Secondary endpoint-conditioned relation

Endpoint matching gives a much weaker, mixed signal:

- tail p: **0.0572**

- rho p: **0.00975**

This does not determine the primary verdict.

## What the experiment has ruled out

The result rules out the specific model:

> lower whole-source pair density causes the local matching law simply to weaken and reverse.

That was the lace-derived hypothesis.

It failed on untouched evidence.

## What remains alive

The preregistered reversal diagnostic suggests a different architecture:

> **low pair density may mark a regime in which more than one lawful local branch becomes possible, while another unmeasured whole-level condition selects the branch.**

That is not yet a discovered law.

It is the question generated by the failed experiment.

The key distinction is:

```text
density -> strength/sign
```

failed.

But:

```text
density -> probability of branch/reversal
```

survived as a secondary signal.

The next opening is provenance on the already-frozen low-density/reversal sources to ask what distinguishes the negative branch from the positive branch **within the same low-density regime**.

No alternative topology predictor was opened before this blind result was frozen.
