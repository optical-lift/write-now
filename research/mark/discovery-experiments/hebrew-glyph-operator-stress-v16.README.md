# Hebrew ↔ Glyph Operator Stress Test v16

V16 attacks the strongest obvious alternative explanations for V15 before any semantic interpretation is attempted.

It keeps the source custody and cross-corpus freeze/evaluation discipline of V15, but removes all boundary-padded states and END outcomes; admits only segments of length 6–10; caps every accepted segment at three deterministic internal events; collapses the consequence to the next item's relation to the current/previous four items only; excludes Hebrew candidate operators that receive any proper-noun morphology tag in train; and requires candidate Hebrew↔glyph pairs to occupy nearly the same within-corpus operator-frequency rank before similarity can even be considered.

Morphology is used solely as an exclusion control. It is not part of the Hebrew fingerprint and cannot increase a Hebrew↔glyph similarity score.

A positive result would mean the V15 correspondence survives these specific structural confound controls. It would still not identify glyph meanings, establish Hebrew translation, prove historical contact, or establish theological or technological claims.
