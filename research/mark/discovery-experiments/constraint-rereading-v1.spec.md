# Mark Constraint Re-Reading v1

Status: **instrument for re-reading the frozen harvest**

## Question

For each canonical active RLOP packet, what is the strongest constraint-shaped statement its own evidence supports?

The re-reading uses the newly clarified form:

> **Given the relevant relational condition, what cannot coexist, cannot be discarded, cannot be substituted, cannot remain invariant, or cannot be inferred without consequence?**

This checkpoint does **not** compare packets to one another and does not assign universal-law identities.

## Critical distinction: reality constraints vs model constraints

Not every negative experimental result is a law of reality.

The instrument therefore separates five levels:

- **observed_system** — a constraint demonstrated in the observed physical/structural system;
- **representation** — a constraint on a coordinate system, compression, classifier or feature representation;
- **measurement** — a constraint caused by censoring, missing contrast or inadequate controls;
- **cross_system** — a constraint on a proposed correspondence between independently represented systems;
- **instrument_calibration** — a property of a deliberately planted calibration world.

Only an `observed_system` record is automatically eligible to become a future law candidate. Other levels may support, constrain or falsify a later law, but they must not be promoted merely because they can be phrased with “cannot.”

## Constraint kinds

- **exclusion** — under condition C, outcome Y is suppressed/forbidden relative to the frozen comparison;
- **dependency** — consequence cannot be specified to the observed resolution without variable/relation X;
- **non_equivalence** — A cannot be substituted, collapsed or identified with B without losing established structure;
- **invariance** — changing a surface/path variable cannot change the consequence within the demonstrated subset;
- **composition** — multiple physical steps are constrained by a composed consequence relation;
- **boundary** — an observation/documentary boundary cannot be assumed to equal a process boundary;
- **underdetermination** — the evidence cannot distinguish the candidate claim;
- **transfer_limit** — a rule/model cannot be carried wholesale into another substrate/system;
- **population_dependence** — a coordinate/border cannot be treated as population-independent.

## Eligibility rule

A constraint is `eligible` only when the source packet demonstrates a relation in the observed system itself.

A constraint is `supporting_only` when it constrains the shape of a future law but is primarily a representation or cross-system result.

A constraint is `deferred` when the experiment is unresolved/underidentified.

A constraint is `ineligible` when it is only instrument calibration or a measurement impossibility.

## Preservation rule

Every projection retains the epistemic ceiling of its source RLOP packet.

The re-reading must never turn:

- statistical suppression into absolute impossibility;
- predictive dependence into causal necessity;
- failure of a model into absence of a real relation;
- correspondence into provenance;
- an artifact of representation into an ontological law.

## No matching

This checkpoint performs **no cross-source equivalence matching**.

Two constraint records may look similar. That similarity is not adjudicated here.

The next law-equivalence instrument remains responsible for deciding whether separately recovered constraints are the same law.
