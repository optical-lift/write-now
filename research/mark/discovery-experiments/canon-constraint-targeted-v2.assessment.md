## Purpose

v1 preserved five genuine no-matches. v2 was allowed to search only for those five.

The goal was not to improve the global match rate. It was to ask whether Noel had simply failed to reconstruct canon rules already present in its source material.

## New canon rules recovered

### TCIR-001 — Connection/membership relation can change while participant identity persists

Evidence:

- Romans 11: branches broken off, wild branch grafted in, natural branches grafted back;
- John 15: branch fruit-bearing depends on remaining in the vine;
- 1 Corinthians 5 / Torah removal formula: membership can change from inside to outside.

Constraint:

> participant identity is not equivalent to connection/membership state.

This gives canon-side structure for connection/topology edits without asserting a universal graph-edit vocabulary.

### TCIR-002 — Member role/function is constrained by placement in a larger relational whole

Evidence:

- 1 Corinthians 12 / Romans 12: many members, one body, God places each member, differentiated function, mutual responsibility;
- Numbers 2: membership has assigned spatial/order consequences around the dwelling;
- nested body/member dwelling observations in Noel.

Constraint:

> isolated member identity is insufficient to specify role/function; placement in the whole carries additional relational information.

This does not prove hidden-state reconstruction from neighbors, but it supplies the missing relational architecture.

### TCIR-003 — Persistence of the same governing condition preserves operation across duration

Evidence:

- Numbers 9:20-23 source-text spine;
- the cloud-abides condition-first rule.

The source text explicitly varies duration across a few days, day/night, two days, a month, or a longer days-period while the governing cloud condition remains. The camp remains camped and does not set out until the cloud lifts.

Constraint:

> changing duration alone cannot change the operation while the governing condition remains unchanged.

This is not generic operation idempotence.
