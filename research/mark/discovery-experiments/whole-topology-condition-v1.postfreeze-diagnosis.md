## The simpler omission is already in the compiler

The frozen null generator groups centers by:

`(center kind, degree)`

before redistributing arm tokens.

So the instrument itself already treats **degree** as a condition that must be preserved.

But the learned grammar context is only:

`CENTER:<kind>|ARM:<state>`

It throws degree away.

That means the current law is aggregating over multiple junction degrees even though the null knows they are different structural states.

This creates a direct candidate for the contradiction:

> **the apparent source-level matching law may actually be a family of degree-conditioned laws whose mixture differs by source.**

No semantic or provenance variable is needed to state that hypothesis.
