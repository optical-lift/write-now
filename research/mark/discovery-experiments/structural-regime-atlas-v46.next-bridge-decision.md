# Mark V46 next-bridge decision record

V46 was designed to choose where to build next rather than force a predetermined endpoint.

## Evidence-ranked directions

### 1. CROSS_SCALE_ORGANIZATION — PRIMARY NEXT BRIDGE

**Evidence for**

- The five anonymous structural regimes reproduce on unseen validation and confirmation sources without refit.
- Regime identity is not reducible to proposal scale.
- Every common regime occurs across multiple proposal scales.
- Real sources contain mixtures of regimes rather than one regime per source.
- The source-relative R4 representation was internally stable but substantially different from the global R3 atlas, indicating a second within-source axis.
- Post-freeze visual/context inspection suggests the regimes are generic structural modes rather than semantic classes.

**Evidence against / limits**

- V46 did not yet construct a frozen parent/child observation relation.
- No transition statistic has been scored.
- Proposal regions may overlap without constituting a true hierarchy.

**Unresolved question**

Do structural modes transform across local -> neighborhood -> field -> object scale according to recurrent source-independent transition rules?

**Smallest discriminating experiment**

Freeze a deterministic geometric containment/overlap graph among existing observations, treat RG-001..RG-005 as an anonymous state alphabet, and test whether cross-scale transition sequences recur and predict parent regimes beyond scale-conditioned marginals.

### 2. HIGHER_ORDER_SEPARATION — COUPLED SECONDARY DIRECTION

**Evidence for**

- V45's two-step physical composition is real but generic across semantic material.
- V46 finds stable larger structural modes.
- A composition of regime transitions could be the next layer above individual V45 operators.
- The user's governing research objective is to discover larger organization rather than prematurely assign meanings.

**Evidence against / limits**

- Exact V45 operator ecology is not present in the V46 feature compiler.
- Regime transitions are not yet equivalent to V45 operator sequences.

**Unresolved question**

Do recurrent multi-scale regime sequences behave like higher-order programs whose organization is more specific than the underlying generic graph machinery?

**Smallest discriminating experiment**

After freezing pairwise cross-scale transitions, compare length-2/3/4 regime paths against source- and scale-preserving nulls and measure out-of-source predictability.

### 3. UNIVERSAL_SUBSTRATE — RETAINED FOUNDATION

V45 CR-001 and V46's cross-media regime recurrence support a lower structural substrate. This is retained as infrastructure rather than treated as the end of the research path.

### 4. REGIME_SPECIFIC_OPERATOR_VOCABULARY — DEFERRED

This cannot be tested faithfully until the exact V5/V45 operator projector is restored or rebuilt under a new frozen equivalence contract.

### 5. UNEXPLAINED_RESIDUAL — RETAINED

The orthogonal source-relative R4 structure and the missing exact V45 ecology remain important residuals. They should be preserved rather than absorbed into the transition experiment.

## Decision

The next experiment should not ask what RG-001 through RG-005 mean.

It should ask:

> **What grammar governs movement among these already-confirmed structural modes as physical structure is viewed at successively larger scales?**

The next bridge is therefore a **Regime Transition Grammar** experiment.

The intended inference direction is:

`confirmed structural mode -> cross-scale transition -> recurrent transition program -> next layer`

not:

`known semantic category -> choose transitions that support it`.
